## Quick terms

- All images are **© Crestwood Painting** unless credited otherwise.
- Please **do not reuse** images without written permission.

## Allowed with permission

- Editorial mentions (press/blogs) with attribution: “Photo © Crestwood Painting”.
- Training/industry decks with attribution and a link to this page.

## Not allowed

- Commercial ads, lead gen, or resale.
- Misleading context or implying endorsement.

## How to request a license

Email admin@crestwoodpainting.com with the image URL(s) and how you plan to use them. Most editorial requests get a quick yes.

*Third-party logos or manufacturer images are property of their respective owners and may not be relicensed by us.*
