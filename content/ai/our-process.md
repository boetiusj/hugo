Crestwood Painting makes home painting hassle-free. Since 2007 we’ve focused on clear communication, reliable schedules, and clean job sites.

## 1) Estimate & Scheduling
- On-site visit or photos, then a clear written proposal
- Firm start window and daily schedule expectations

## 2) Color & Materials
- Guidance on sheen and durability
- Premium paints from Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore

## 3) Jobsite Protection
- Floors covered, furnishings protected, dust control in place
- Safe, orderly work areas

## 4) Prep
- Fill, sand, and caulk where appropriate
- Spot-prime as needed for a uniform finish

## 5) Paint
- Skilled application for walls, trim, doors, cabinets, and exteriors
- Consistent finish across surfaces

## 6) Daily Cleanup
- Tools organized, floors cleared, walkways open
- Home stays livable throughout

## 7) Final Walkthrough
- Touch-ups and quality check together
- Warranty and care tips provided

## Why It Works
A predictable, tidy process delivers durable results—and a smoother customer experience.
