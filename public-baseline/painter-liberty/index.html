Professional Painting in Liberty

Crestwood Painting brings expert craftsmanship and lasting results to Overland Park homes. Whether refreshing interiors, reviving exteriors, or updating cabinets, our skilled team delivers a smooth, worry-free experience.

Interior Painting

We bring precision and durability to every space, from living rooms to kitchens. Explore our services:

Plaster & Drywall Repair – Flawless starts here

Ceilings and walls aren’t immune to daily wear and tear. From bathroom moisture damage to settling cracks, we repair and reinforce problem areas. Your walls will look fresh, sturdy, and ready for years to come.

Ceiling Painting – A fresh coat overhead

A yellowed or stained ceiling can make a room feel darker than it should. We take care of stains, cracks, and repairs before applying a smooth, even coat of paint. The result is a crisp, clean ceiling that enhances the whole space.

Cabinet Painting – A kitchen upgrade without the remodel

New cabinets are expensive, but a fresh paint job delivers the same impact for much less. Durable finishes are designed to withstand everyday use while giving your kitchen or bath a fresh, modern look. It’s an easy way to update your home without the stress of remodeling.

Basement Painting – Brighten the space below

A simple coat of paint can turn your basement into a stylish and functional extension of your home. Light colors create an airy feel, while bold choices bring energy to media rooms or play spaces. A professional paint job ensures it looks great and lasts for years.

Wallpaper Removal – Clean slate, done right

Wallpaper removal is harder than it looks—unless you call us. Our wallpaper removal pros deliver smooth walls and a fresh, modern appearance with zero hassle.

Bathroom Painting – A fresh look, even in high humidity

A clean, well-painted bathroom makes a big difference in your home’s overall feel. Moisture-resistant paint helps prevent stains and peeling, keeping walls in top shape. A fresh color update gives your bathroom a crisp, refreshed look.

Living Room Painting – Set the tone for your home

Over time, walls can lose their luster, making a space feel dull. A new coat of paint refreshes the room, adding warmth and personality. With the right color and finish, your living room becomes a space you love to relax in.

Playroom Painting – Color that can keep up

A playroom should be as lively or as calming as your child needs. Bold colors bring energy, while gentle pastels create a soothing atmosphere. With high-quality, washable paint, the room stays looking great no matter how much fun takes place.

Exterior Painting in Liberty

A well-painted home exterior makes a statement before you even step inside. It’s about beauty, protection, and lasting quality. Here’s what we can do to make your home stand out:

Front Doors – Make an entrance that gets noticed

Your door deserves more than just a coat of paint—it needs presence. We’ll help you choose a bold color that works with your home’s style. The result? A front door that feels custom and completely you.

Garage Painting

We accurately match your home’s colors for a seamless appearance. From doors to siding, we’ll make sure the finish is sharp and smooth. Because yes, your garage deserves the same attention as the rest of the house.

Playset Painting – Give the backyard a cheerful upgrade

A freshly painted playset looks great and stays protected from harsh weather, preventing wood rot or rust on metal frames. Durable paints ensure a long-lasting finish, while bright colors make the playset more inviting and fun. Whether wooden or metal, professional painting extends its life and keeps it safe for years of enjoyment.

Gazebo Painting – Protection meets polish

We help you enhance your gazebo with color, coverage, and care. Our paints and stains protect wood from sun, rain, and seasonal shifts. The result is a space that feels as good as it looks.

We have peace of mind knowing we can call a contractor that we can trust to be timely, reliable, accurate, clean, tidy, efficient and affordable.

Fence Staining – Rich color, real protection

Our stains soak in deep to preserve and protect, not just coat the surface. The result is a consistent, rich color that holds up through Kansas City’s changing seasons. Your fence will thank you.

Metal Fence Painting – A clean look that lasts

Metal fences need more than paint—they need protection. We apply long-lasting finishes that keep rust and wear at bay. It's all about keeping your fence solid and stylish.
