# Plaster and Drywall Repair | Crestwood Painting

Before paint, walls need to be smooth and sound. Crestwood Painting repairs cracks, patches holes, and prepares plaster or drywall for a lasting finish.

## What We Do
- Plaster crack repair and skim coating
- Drywall patching and sanding
- Surface prep before painting

## Our Approach
- Clean, dust-controlled repairs
- Blending repairs seamlessly with existing surfaces
- Ready-to-paint finishes with no shortcuts

## Why It Matters
A strong paint job starts with a solid surface. Crestwood Painting ensures walls look as good as new.
